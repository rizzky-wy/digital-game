Communication & Presentation: Sides & Angles

Let's present our findings to the class! We will explore in depth the two main components of 2D shapes: Sides (Edges) and Angles (Corners).

Slide 1 of 4

What is a Side (Edge)?

A side is a straight or curved line segment that forms the outer boundary of a 2D shape. For example, a square has 4 straight boundary lines.

What is an Angle (Corner)?

An angle is formed where two straight sides meet at a corner point. A square has 4 corner angles where its sides join.

Guide to Making Shape Sentences

  • Name the 2D shape (e.g., Square, Triangle).
  • State the number of sides (e.g., It has 4 sides).
  • State the number of angles or special feature (e.g., It has 4 right angles).
  • Give an everyday object example (e.g., like a floor tile).
"Example Sentence: A notebook is shaped like a rectangle because it has 4 sides and 4 angles."
